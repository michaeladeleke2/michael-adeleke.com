## What the activity asked

Twenty items, each offering four ways to finish a sentence about how I lecture. Distribute five points across the four options on every item. The hundred points total across four lecture styles.

## What I made

## What I learned

I describe myself to other people as a Kahoot teacher. Energizer, the style that runs on enthusiasm and fun, is my lowest score by a wide margin, and Fascinator is nearly double the next one.

Looking back at my own item responses explains it rather than contradicting it. I gave four of five points to live demonstration, and four to knowledge sharer, and nothing at all to jokes or fun activities. The Kahoot is not where my energy comes from. It is an instrument I use to find out whether the room is with me, which is a different thing, and I had been describing it as the first while using it as the second.

## How this shapes my teaching

Fascinator at forty-four against Energizer at seven names my likeliest failure mode precisely: a well organised session that nobody is excited by. That is useful, because it means the Kahoot is doing work my natural style does not do on its own, and I should keep it for that reason rather than drop it when a session runs short.

It also means I should stop treating the Kahoot as evidence that the room is engaged. It tells me what students know. Whether they care is a separate question, and on this evidence it is not one my instincts will answer for me.
