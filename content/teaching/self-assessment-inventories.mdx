## What the activity asked

Complete four Cross Academy self assessments and consider what they say about my instructional preferences, strengths, and areas for improvement: the Engaged Teaching Inventory, the Instructor Motivation Quiz, the Lecture Style Inventory, and You Might Be a Constructivist If.

## What I made

Engaged Teaching Inventory: Facilitator 8.75, Personal Model 7.75, Delegator 7, Expert 6.75, Formal Authority 6.25. Instructor Motivation Quiz: Influence 30, Enlightenment 20, and zero for Connection, Security and Accomplishment. Lecture Style Inventory: Fascinator 44, Performer 25, Inspirer 24, Energizer 7. You Might Be a Constructivist If: Cognitivism 3, Behaviorism 2, Constructivism 2, Humanism 0.

## What I learned

Taken together the four results are more useful for their contradictions than for their headline scores. Facilitator was my highest teaching style at 8.75, and that style is defined by the personal nature of teacher and student interactions, yet Connection scored zero on the motivation quiz, meaning I prefer the pedagogy of connection without being motivated by connection itself. The second contradiction is sharper: I describe myself as a Kahoot style teacher, but Energizer was my lowest lecture style at 7, well behind Fascinator at 44. My own item responses explain it, since I gave four points to live demonstration and four to knowledge sharer but nothing to jokes or fun activities, which means the Kahoot is functioning as a measurement instrument for me rather than as an energy source. The third result is the most consistent: Fascinator at nearly double the next score, Personal Model second among teaching styles, and Cognitivism as my top learning theory all describe a teacher organized around conveying a clear process and modelling it, which is the watch then do structure I keep building. The warning sits in the Personal Model description itself, that students who deviate from the instructor's preferred approach may feel inadequate, and debugging is a topic where many valid routes exist.

## How this shapes my teaching

The Personal Model risk is the one to act on first, and the debugging plan already has the fix in it: the debrief question that compares different debugging approaches. That is the block I was about to shorten for time, and it is the one block that protects students whose process does not look like mine, so it stays. The Connection result tells me not to assume I will naturally invest in office hours and relationship building, so I should schedule that structurally rather than rely on a motivation I do not appear to have. Cognitivism as my top theory means I will default to sequence, structure and cognitive load, which serves intro CS well, and that the affective side is what I will underweight, which is also where I got the defeated novice case wrong. Fascinator at 44 against Energizer at 7 names my likeliest failure mode as a well organized session that nobody is excited by, which is useful to know, because it means the Kahoot is doing work my natural style does not.
